# Med Payment Tech Site

Build a modern, highly responsive, professional landing page web app for a medical payment processing company using Tailwind CSS and React.

### General Information & Domain

- Domain: MedPaymentTech.com

- Main Title / Branding: Capital PaymentTech | Clover PracticePay

### Visual Style & Color Palette

- Style: Clean, professional, trustworthy, modern healthcare-fintech aesthetics.

- Background Colors: Light gray/off-white background (#F8FAFC / #F1F5F9) with crisp white cards (#FFFFFF).

- Primary Accent Colors: 

  - Deep Navy Blue (#0A2540 or #1E293B) for primary text, main headings, dark footers, and prominent cards.

  - Emerald Green (#10B981 or #059669) for highlights, green accents, checkmark icons, and call-to-action buttons.

- Typography: Sans-serif font (e.g., Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans), clean, high readability, bold headings.

- Layout: Structured multi-column grid layouts with clean iconography, rounded borders, and soft box-shadows.

### Page Structure & Order of Sections (Follow this exact sequence)

#### 1. Header / Navigation Bar

- Top Branding Logos: "CAPITAL PaymentTech" alongside "Clover PracticePay".

- Include a subtle top banner or badge text: "Healthier Practices, Brighter Tomorrows — Patients, People, Healthier Communities".

#### 2. Hero Section

- Main Headline: "A HEALTHIER WAY TO GET PAID" (Bold, Navy Blue & Green accents).

- Subheadline: "Complete payment solutions for modern medical practices. Lower processing costs. More patient payment options. A stronger, more profitable practice."

- Hero Visual / Card: On the right side, show a featured card with a medical professional (doctor/nurse) wearing a stethoscope.

- Callout Badge in Hero: "MEDICAL PRACTICE SAVINGS GUARANTEE - See how much your practice could save."

#### 3. Core Features / Key Benefits Grid (5 Columns / Cards)

Display 5 feature cards in a grid layout with green icons:

1. Surcharge Program: "Pass credit card fees legally and easily." (Icon: Credit card / coins)

2. Low Debit Rates: "Keep costs low on debit transactions." (Icon: POS Card terminal)

3. Next-Day Funding: "Improve your cash flow." (Icon: Lightning bolt)

4. HSA/FSA Ready: "Accept HSA/FSA payments with ease." (Icon: Heart in shield / medical heart)

5. Payment Links: "Collect balances anytime, anywhere." (Icon: Mobile phone / link)

#### 4. Additional Benefits & Complimentary Equipment Section

- Left Column (List of 8 Benefits with icons):

  1. Recurring Payments – Ideal for memberships, treatment plans, and follow-ups.

  4. Dedicated Account Manager – Personal support, not a call center.

  6. Staff Training Included – For a smooth implementation.

  8. Annual Savings Report – See exactly how much you've saved.

- Right Column (Featured Card):

  - Card Title: "Complimentary Equipment"

  - Card Body: "Clover devices at no upfront cost."

  - Image/Mockup placeholder: Modern Clover POS terminal device with receipt text "Payments Made Simple for a Healthier Tomorrow".
